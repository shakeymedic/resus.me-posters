THE W.O.B.B.L.E.R.

If the patient looks well but the ECG wobbles, admit for telemetry.

W

WPW

Pre-excitation

Delta wave. Short PR (<120ms). Risk of SVT → VF.

O

OBSTRUCTION

AV Block

PR >200ms? Mobitz? Axis deviation suggests conduction disease.

B

BIFASCICULAR

Major Block

RBBB + Left Axis (LAFB) or Right Axis (LPFB). High risk of CHB.

B

BRUGADA

Channelopathy

V1-V2: Coved ST elevation >2mm (Type 1). Saddleback (Type 2).

L

LVH

Hypertrophy

Deep Q waves (HOCM). Severe Aortic Stenosis strain pattern.

E

EPSILON

ARVC

Notch at end of QRS in V1-V3. T-wave inversion V1-V3.

R

REPOLARISATION

QT Interval

Long QT (QTc >450ms). Short QT (<330ms). R-on-T phenomenon.

The Whimperative

"Mitigated speech" creates ambiguity when clarity is vital. It is a disaster in resuscitation.

"Would someone mind maybe grabbing the airway trolley?"

Fail: Ambiguous Target

"Any chance we could get a line in?"

Fail: Question vs Order

"I think we should probably shock him soon."

Fail: Hinting vs Action

The Directive

Be polite, but be direct. Use names. Use close-looped communication.

"Steve, bring the airway trolley to the bedside."

Name + Action + Location

"Sarah, insert a large-bore IV in the left ACF."

Name + Specific Task

"Charge the defibrillator. Everyone stand clear."

Clear Command

Clinical Trajectory

When resuscitation fails, do not just "work harder" at the bottom of the pyramid. Stop. Move UP a level.

Why?

The Failure Trap

We often blame "Skills" first. But usually, the failure is higher up. Is the logistics (position) wrong? Is the strategy (plan) wrong?

S.T.E.P.U.P.

The Zero Point Survey

"30 seconds of preparation before the patient arrives is worth 30 minutes of chaos afterwards."

SELF

"I am calm. I am ready."

  • • Cognitive unloading (Step back)
  • • PPE check (Safety first)
  • • Stress control breathing

TEAM

"I am Team Leader."

  • • Identify Team Leader (Vest on)
  • • Assign roles (Airway, Access, Drugs)
  • • Stickers on / Introductions made

ENVIRONMENT

"Is the room ready?"

  • • Trolley height set & brakes on
  • • Airway plan (A, B, C kit out)
  • • Ultrasound machine on & gel ready

PATIENT

"What do we know?"

  • • ATMIST Summary review
  • • Age / Weight (Broselow/WETFLAG)
  • • Specific hazards (Chem/Rad/Bio)

UPDATE

"Here is the plan..."

  • • Shared Mental Model Briefing
  • • "We are expecting X"
  • • "If X happens, we will do Y"

PRIORITIES

"The first action is..."

  • • Immediate IV access?
  • • Finger thoracostomy?
  • • Transfer to CT (Don't take off scoop)?
